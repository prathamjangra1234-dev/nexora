01 / The challenge

The challenge

This concept asks how a neighbourhood food market could feel both practical and full of character. The identity needs to make browsing simple without smoothing away the human details that make a local shop memorable.

02 / The approach

The approach

Build a flexible visual language around field notes, growing things and clear product information. Keep the tone warm and direct, with enough structure to move between packaging, a storefront and everyday communication.

03 / The execution

The execution

The exploration pairs a typographic identity with a small digital storefront direction. It is a studio concept, not commissioned client work.
