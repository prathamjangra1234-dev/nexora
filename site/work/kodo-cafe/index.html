KODO Café

Twenty pieces from one playful visual universe—social moments, campaign posters, product stories and café life.

Project type

Role

Services
Campaign artwork · Social creative · Graphic design
Social creative · from the 20-piece collection

01 / The challenge

The challenge

The collection explores a familiar territory—the coffee break—through twenty different visual moments. The challenge within the work was keeping its warmth recognizable while each piece carried a different message.

02 / The approach

The approach

Move between product studies, playful lines, local food stories, familiar café scenes and pop-culture ideas. Each piece gets its own focal point while the full collection keeps a shared sense of colour, humour and place.

03 / The execution

The execution

The archive brings together campaign posters, product visuals, café stories and social mock-ups. The featured image is one frame from the collection; the complete set remains browsable on the Work page.
