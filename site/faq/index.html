01

Working together

What does NEXORA work on?+

The studio works across brand and graphic design, websites and digital products, social media and campaigns, video and motion, and practical AI or automation. We shape the team around the work rather than asking every project to fit the same package.

Who is a good fit for NEXORA?+

Teams with a meaningful problem to solve and a willingness to work through it together. You can arrive with a detailed brief or an early question; the first conversation helps us understand whether the studio is the right partner.

Can you join partway through a project?+

Yes. Tell us what is already in motion, what is working and where you need support. We will agree a clear role and handoff with your team before work begins.

02

Projects and process

How does a project get started?+

Send a short brief through the project form. We read it, follow up with useful questions and, when the fit is right, outline the scope, deliverables, timing and next steps before anything is booked.

How do you scope and price work?+

Scope depends on the problem, the people involved and the deliverables needed. After an initial conversation, we propose a clear scope and fee for review. There is no obligation to proceed until the details are agreed.

How will I review work and share feedback?+

We agree review points and a communication rhythm at the start. Work is shared in stages, with a clear moment for feedback and decisions so the project keeps moving without surprises.

Can the scope change once work begins?+

If the brief changes, we talk through its impact on deliverables and timing before adjusting the plan. That keeps decisions visible for everyone involved.

03

After launch

What happens when the project is finished?+

We hand over the agreed files, documentation and access details. If ongoing creative, development or operational support would help, we can discuss it as a separate scope.
